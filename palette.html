How to read these: the colour only ever appears in small doses — tiny caps, one rule, a hover, one honour line. So judge the whisper, not the swatch. All options pass accessibility contrast on this background. Champagne is the quiet evolution of the current amber; oxblood and blush lean costume-house; verdigris and eucalyptus pick up the film stills' landscapes; slate reads editorial; ivory is the severe fashion answer — no colour at all, letting the photography carry everything. — J&V pick one, or say what's missing and we mix it.
